Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Onyx collects no data about you. There's no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no tracking. The developer has no servers and never receives your subscriptions, listening history, clips, transcripts, or files.

What stays on your device

Your subscriptions, queue, listening progress, downloads, clips, notes, transcripts, summaries, chapters, and imported files are stored on your iPhone. Transcription, clip titles and summaries, chapters, and recommendations are all worked out on the device. Widgets read a small summary that the app saves on your iPhone.

Connections Onyx makes

Onyx connects only to the following, and only to do what you asked:

  • Podcast hosts. To refresh your shows and stream or download episodes, Onyx fetches feeds and audio directly from the servers that host them, as any podcast app does. Those servers see your IP address and the episode you request, under their own privacy policies.
  • Apple's podcast directory. Searching for shows sends your search words to Apple's public directory. Recommendations request Apple's public top charts by category and country; nothing from your library is sent.
  • Hugging Face. If you choose to install an optional on-device model, Onyx downloads it from Hugging Face. Nothing about you or your library is sent.
  • iCloud Drive. If you choose to keep books and files in iCloud Drive, or save backups there, Apple stores them in your own iCloud account.

Backups and exports

Backups, exported clips, and notes go only where you send them: a folder you pick, the share sheet, or another app such as Obsidian.

Notifications

New-episode notifications are created on your device. Onyx doesn't use a push notification service.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. Onyx only learns whether a purchase is active; it never sees your payment details.

Children

Onyx doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
