Support

Need help or found a bug?
Email onyxpodcast@icloud.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing. The Onyx version is in Settings → About.

Common questions

How do I save a clip with my headphones?

While a podcast is playing, triple-press the button on wired headphones, or press the stem of your AirPods three times. This works with the phone locked, and playback keeps going. You can also use the clip button in the player, the widget, or the Control Center control.

Why doesn't a clip have a summary?

Titles and summaries are made by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone, so they need a device that supports it, with Apple Intelligence turned on. Apple's safety filter sometimes declines a passage; the clip keeps a plain title and its reason is shown in the clip's details.

How do I send clips to my notes app?

Open a clip and share it as Markdown, or pick a folder as the Notes Folder in Settings → Export so notes are written there directly. Each note links back to the moment in the episode.

Where are my downloads and files?

In the Files app, under On My iPhone → Onyx: Podcasts, Books, and My Files. You can move Books and My Files to iCloud Drive in Settings → Onyx Folder.

How do I back up or move to a new iPhone?

Settings → Backup & Restore makes a backup file, and can save one automatically each day to a folder you pick. On the new iPhone, open the backup file with Onyx to restore. Restoring adds to your library and never deletes anything.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings → Onyx Unlock and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Does Onyx collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy.
