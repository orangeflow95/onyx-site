Keep the moments worth keeping.

Onyx is a podcast player built around clips. Save a moment with one tap or a headphone press, and Onyx transcribes and summarizes it on your iPhone.

Clips

Capture from the player, your headphones, a widget, or the lock screen. Playback never stops.

Transcripts

Full-episode transcripts you can search, made on your device.

Titles and summaries

Each clip gets a title and a short summary, made by on-device models.

Export

Share clips as audio or Markdown notes for Obsidian and Logseq.

Chapters

Chapters from the show, or generated from the transcript.

Your own files

Play audiobooks, recordings, and videos alongside your podcasts.

No account, no tracking, no ads. Your listening stays on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
